Music, named in data/sounds.json "music": OGG files (WAV is read too; an .ogg wins if both are there).

tools/sound/pieces.py (v0.103.0: the_tower for the title screen, the_brisering, and miasma_wilds,
miasma_deep, miasma_stone and miasma_mire for hunts). The user's own music replaces them.
The stingers for big cards are in data/sounds/ (st_*.ogg).
